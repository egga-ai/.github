**EGGA is a growth, sales and marketing service for B2B SMEs, run for you on one fixed monthly fee.** We sharpen your offer, put you in front of buyers with budget and give your sales team what it needs to close. *The unfair operating advantage.*

The work covers four areas, every month:

- **Direction.** Where to grow and who to sell to, set out in a 90-day growth plan.
- **Visibility.** Found on Google, in AI answers such as ChatGPT, and on your own website.
- **Customers.** Lead generation, campaigns and follow-up that turn attention into conversations and closed work.
- **Scale.** New markets and languages, bigger accounts, and a monthly report on what moved.

EGGA works with owner-led and founder-led B2B companies, from a global site and country versions for [Singapore](https://egga.ai/sg) and [India](https://egga.ai/in). It is operated by Acchalabs Pte. Ltd., Singapore.
