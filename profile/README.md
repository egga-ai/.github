## Frequently asked questions

### What is EGGA?
EGGA is a growth, sales and marketing service for B2B SMEs, sold as one fixed monthly subscription. Strategy and execution sit in the same plan: positioning and offer, visibility on Google and in AI answers, lead generation and campaigns, sales materials and a monthly report. Its website is [egga.ai](https://egga.ai).

### Who is EGGA for?
Owner-led and founder-led B2B companies with a real growth opportunity and a decision-maker ready to move. Typical clients are manufacturers, distributors, industrial and professional services firms and B2B technology companies that are outgrowing referrals, entering a new market or preparing to sell or scale.

### How is EGGA different from a marketing agency?
Agencies usually sell deliverables. EGGA starts from the business, agrees one commercial goal and ties every piece of work to it, with one dedicated contact and a monthly report on what moved.

### How much does EGGA cost?
There are three plans, Base, Core and Full, each at one fixed monthly fee with no setup fee. Prices are published in full on the [EGGA pricing page](https://egga.ai/pricing), with country versions for [Singapore](https://egga.ai/sg/pricing) and [India](https://egga.ai/in/pricing). Monthly plans cancel with 30 days' notice.

### Does EGGA help companies show up in ChatGPT and AI search?
Yes. AI search visibility is one of EGGA's services: content, pages and signals that help ChatGPT, other AI assistants and Google understand a company and name it when buyers ask.
